✨ What Can This App Do?

Everything you need to manage student marks — designed so even a school student can use it!

🧑‍🎓

Student Registration

Add students from 10 engineering branches — CSE, IT, Mechanical, Civil, ECE and more. First Year to B.Tech Final Year.

📝

Marks Entry

Enter marks for 5 subjects — Test 1 (20 marks), Test 2 (20 marks), Final Exam (60 marks). Total = 100 per subject.

📊

Auto Grading

Grades calculated automatically — O, A+, A, B+, B, C, or F. Pass/Fail shown instantly for each subject.

📄

Marksheet Report

Beautiful marksheet with subject-wise breakdown, totals, percentage, and visual progress bars.

🤖

AI Analysis (OpenCode)

Get AI-powered performance analysis, detect weak subjects, and receive personalized improvement tips!

🔄

Multi-Model Comparison

Compare reports from different AI models — OpenAI GPT, Ollama (local), and our built-in analyzer.
