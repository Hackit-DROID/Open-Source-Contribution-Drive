┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  METHOD 1: Download Individual Files (EASIEST)                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Download these 3 essential files to a folder on your computer:

  ✅ index.html  (Main application file)
  ✅ styles.css  (Styling file)
  ✅ app.js      (JavaScript logic)

Location: /home/user/student-mis/

Then:
  1. Keep all 3 files in the SAME folder
  2. Double-click "index.html" to open in browser
  3. That's it! The app will work perfectly!

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  METHOD 2: Download Complete Archive                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Download this file:
  📦 /home/user/student-mis.tar.gz

Then:
  1. Extract the archive
  2. Open the "student-mis" folder
  3. Double-click "index.html"
  4. Enjoy your application!
